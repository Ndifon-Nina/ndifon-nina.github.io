💰 Try to get your money back

MTN Mobile Money self-reversal

  • Dial *126#
  • Choose option 7 "My account"
  • Choose option 5 "Reverse Transaction"
  • Select the transaction and follow the prompts
  • No confirmation? Call 8787 or WhatsApp 683 00 8787

Orange Money reversal

  • Dial 950 within 48 hours of the transaction
  • Choose your language
  • Select the wrongful Mobile Money transaction option
  • Choose "Speak to an agent" and explain what happened

⚠️ If the receiving account is frozen, you may need a police report to authorise release of the funds; for amounts above 50,000 FCFA, a court order may be required. Your provider's agent will guide you through this if it applies.
